Both placements use the same synthetic baseline and equal default gains: no validated front/back gain ratio is available here. Front/volar placement may be near the radial artery; back/dorsal placement does not share that measurement site. Default 1.5× gain and 5-minute time constant are assumptions. Vessel diameter does not determine this gain. The top plots show synchronized 8-second windows; the baseline continues as a comparison, not a second simultaneous measurement on the same site.

Evidence and model limitations
  • Venous study: basilic vein near the elbow, vascular-access patients, axillary block; 3.0 ± 1.1 to 4.1 ± 1.2 mm. Ratio of group means = +36.7%; reported mean of individual post/pre ratios = 143 ± 34% (mean individual increase +43%). These are different statistics. They are not wrist measurements or one person's trajectory.
  • Arterial study: radial artery near the wrist, women with gestational hypertension, median nerve block. Table 3 reports median diameter changes +22% at 10 min and 0% at 30 min; median CSA changes +50% and 0%. Ratio of diameter group means at 10 min = +20%, a different statistic. The table labels diameter as mm but lists 0.25 → 0.30; this is inconsistent with its CSA values. Do not silently treat those values as verified mm or corrected cm.
  • Default exponential response is an illustrative onset-and-plateau assumption. Linear/logarithmic options saturate at 15 min. Transient peaks at 10 min and returns to baseline at 30 min; its PPG behavior is an assumption inspired by the artery diameter timing, not a PPG fit.
  • Finger perfusion-index research supports increased AC/DC after some successful blocks. It does not validate a fixed wrist amplitude increase. Venous filling can affect DC; DC is not modeled here. No diameter-to-optical transfer model or SpO₂ calibration is included.
  • Signal polarity is chosen for upward displayed pulses. Arbitrary units are not MAX30102 ADC counts. LED drive, ADC range, strap pressure, motion and optical coupling are held constant. No automatic normalization is applied between windows or conditions.
  • The pulse template, respiration, noise and response parameters are synthetic. Healthy normotension does not uniquely specify PPG morphology. Cohort standard deviations are not injected as temporal noise or used as individual uncertainty bounds.
